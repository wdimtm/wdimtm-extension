Privacy

  • Only selected text, page title/URL, and a bounded neighborhood leave the content script.
  • The full page DOM is never uploaded.
  • Mock: explain payloads stay in the browser.
  • BYOK: the explain payload is sent to your configured OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Keys stay in chrome.storage (not product cloud unless you opt in).
  • Agentaab: the explain payload is sent to the Agentaab app endpoint under your subscription token.
  • Local memories live in chrome.storage.local. Nothing is auto-remembered.
  • Images you attach in chat — including a system screenshot you paste in — are sent to the same AI endpoint as the text, and only when you send that message. Full-size image data stays in the tab; only a small thumbnail is kept, in session storage.

How to use

  1. Select text on a page.
  2. Optionally change the Lens on the bubble.
  3. Click WDIMTM.
  4. Use Explain more, Why it matters, Verify, Discuss further, or Remember this.
  5. Press Esc or click outside to dismiss.
