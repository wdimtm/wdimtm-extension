Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

WDIMTM Cloud is our hosted service. Bring your own key sends nothing to us.

What leaves your device

When you ask for an explanation, the extension sends the following to WDIMTM Cloud or to the AI endpoint you configured — and nowhere else:

  • The text you selected.
  • A bounded amount of surrounding page text. The full page is never sent.
  • The page title and URL.
  • The instructions of the lens you are using.
  • Your profile text and any saved memories judged relevant to the selection.

In bring-your-own-key mode that endpoint is whichever provider you point the extension at.

Images you attach in page chat are sent the same way as the text, only when you send that message. The extension never captures your screen itself.

If you turn on web search, the search query is additionally sent to your search provider.

What stays on your device

  • Memories and your profile text are stored in extension local storage.
  • Page chat threads are stored in session storage.
  • Selections themselves are never retained.
  • Images you attach are held in the tab; only a small thumbnail is stored.

Settings sync. API keys do not.

Your preferences are stored in Chrome's synced extension storage.

API keys are deliberately excluded from that.

Importing your chat history

Importing your chat history is always something you start.

  • Read in the page only, never written to disk.
  • Sent to the endpoint you configured, under your key.
  • Nothing is saved until you review and tick it.
  • Closing the page discards the raw conversations.

WDIMTM Cloud

WDIMTM Cloud runs at cloud.wdimtm.com and requires signing in with Google.

What WDIMTM Cloud stores for your account:

  • Your account: email, Google account id, plan, sign-up date.
  • Sign-in sessions, stored only as a hash.
  • A credit ledger of modes, credits, token counts and times — not content.
  • Purchases: credits bought or refunded, and when.
  • If sync is on: your profile, lenses, memories and preferences, never API keys.
  • Research jobs you start, with their context and results.

Explanations and page chat are forwarded to a model and not stored.

Payments are taken by Agentaab and Stripe. We never see your card.

Requests are rate limited by account and IP address.

We keep this data until you delete your account.

In WDIMTM Cloud, web search runs on the Cloud backend and queries go to Tavily on your behalf.

What we do not do

  • In bring-your-own-key mode, we receive no data from the extension.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data.
  • Your data is never used for advertising or model training.
  • The extension reads page content only when you select text and ask.

Your controls

  • View, edit, or delete any memory in Settings → Memory.
  • Set the memory provider to “none” to stop storing memories entirely.
  • Turn WDIMTM off for the site you are on, or add hostnames to the site denylist.
  • Turn the extension off globally, or uninstall it.
  • Export your settings and memories at any time.
  • Download everything WDIMTM Cloud stores for your account.
  • Delete your WDIMTM Cloud account and its data.

Children

WDIMTM is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
